# Example results

Figures are illustrative.

## Inquiry to quote

> Every customer who sends a request for a quote receives a correct quote they can act on within one working day, priced inside the discount rules and above the margin floor.

`Receiver ✓ Solution-free ✓ Stop ✓ Measurable ✓`

[Visual: one bar per measure, today on the left, target on the right. Turnaround: days → 1 day. Quotes checked against the rules: a sample → every quote.]

| Measure | Today | Target | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Turnaround, request to quote | Days | One working day | ERP timestamps |
| Quotes checked against the discount rules | A sample | Every quote | Pricing log |
| Engineer time on standard requests | Every request reviewed | Novel requests only | Time records |
| Share of requests quoted | Small ones declined | Every request | Inbox and CRM |
| Win rate | Unmeasured | Up, a hypothesis | CRM |

Principles: AI prepares, people decide. Visible AI, labelled and logged. Guidance without roadblocks: only the margin floor blocks. Reduce work, don't shift it: engineer time falls and none of it moves to inside sales.

| Red line | Owner |
| --- | --- |
| ⛔ Never quote below the margin floor | Pricing manager |
| ⛔ Discounts stay inside the limits finance sets | Finance |
| ⛔ Every deviation from the specification is signed | Application engineer |
| ⛔ A person signs every price and bid before anything leaves | Sales manager |
