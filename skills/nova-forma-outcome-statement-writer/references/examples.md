## Inquiry to quote

The scope card from step 1 says the redesign must show: engineer hours on standard requests close to zero, every quote checked against the discount rules, small requests quoted instead of declined, and a person still signs every price and bid.

> Every customer who sends a request for a quote receives a correct quote they can act on within one working day, priced inside the discount rules and above the margin floor.

`Receiver ✓ Solution-free ✓ Stop ✓ Measurable ✓`

[Visual: one bar per measure, today on the left, target on the right. Turnaround: days → 1 day. Quotes checked against the rules: a sample → every quote.]

| Measure | Today | Target | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Turnaround, request to quote | Days | One working day | ERP timestamps |
| Quotes checked against the discount rules | A sample | Every quote | Pricing log |
| Engineer hours on standard requests | Unmeasured, every request reviewed | Close to zero | Time records |
| Small requests quoted instead of declined | Most declined | Every one quoted | Inbox and CRM |
| Win rate | Unmeasured | Up, a hypothesis | CRM |

Principles:

- AI prepares, people decide.
- Visible AI, labelled and logged.
- Guidance without roadblocks: only the red lines block.
- Reduce work, don't shift it: engineer time falls and none of it moves to inside sales.

| Red line | Owner |
| --- | --- |
| ⛔ Never quote below the margin floor | Pricing manager |
| ⛔ Discounts above the limit need the sales manager's approval | Sales manager |
| ⛔ Every deviation from the specification is signed | Application engineer |
| ⛔ A person still signs every price and bid before anything leaves | Sales manager |

How we use this: in the redesign week, each new way of working is scored on these measures, and any that breaks a red line is out.
