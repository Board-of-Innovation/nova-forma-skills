# Evidence for the four skills

Verified public findings, checked 30 October 2026.

## Rules

1. Cite only from this file or from sources opened in this session.
2. Give at most two findings per result: one benchmark for the pick, and one test for the user's idea.
3. Name the source in plain words, with institution and year.
4. Never present a vendor claim or prediction as fact. Say "a vendor reports" or "Gartner predicts".
5. When nothing fits, say so. Point the user to their own data: the full-year figures their systems hold, or 20 timed cases where nothing records the timing.
6. Cite a benchmark only when you can say what it measures and when.

## 1. Redesign beats adding tools

E1. About 75% of AI high performers fundamentally redesign workflows, against 25% of other firms.
[McKinsey, The state of AI in 2026, 2026, survey, https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/quantumblack/our-insights/the-state-of-ai]
Use it to: argue for redesigning the whole workflow.

E2. One task fell from ten days to one, yet customers still waited ten days. Nothing around it changed.
[BCG, Look Past Productivity to Get Real Value from AI, 2026, case, https://www.bcg.com/publications/2026/why-ai-pilots-rarely-deliver-value]
Use it to: show one faster step leaves customers waiting.

## 2. Most AI projects fail to pay, and why

E3. Only 37% of firms report any EBIT impact from AI. About 6% get 5% or more of EBIT from it.
[McKinsey, The state of AI in 2026, 2026, survey, https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/quantumblack/our-insights/the-state-of-ai]
Use it to: temper EBIT promises in a value case.

E4. About 95% of firms report zero return on gen AI. Partnered builds reached deployment twice as often as internal ones.
[MIT NANDA, The GenAI Divide, 2025, survey (directional), https://webberwentzel.com/News/Documents/2025/MIT1757411281972.pdf]
Use it to: steer small firms toward partners.

E5. Gartner predicted at least 30% of gen AI projects would be abandoned after proof of concept. Reasons: poor data, weak risk controls, rising costs, unclear value.
[Gartner, Gartner Predicts 30 Percent of Generative AI Projects Will Be Abandoned After Proof of Concept By End of 2025, 2024, prediction, https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2024-07-29-gartner-predicts-30-percent-of-generative-ai-projects-will-be-abandoned-after-proof-of-concept-by-end-of-2025]
Use it to: build kill criteria into every pilot.

## 3. Which work gains most, and the jagged frontier

E6. Inside AI's frontier, consultants did 12% more tasks, 25% faster, at over 40% higher quality. Outside it, correct answers fell 19 points.
[Harvard Business School, Navigating the Jagged Technological Frontier, 2023, study, https://mitsloan.mit.edu/sites/default/files/2023-10/SSRN-id4573321.pdf]
Use it to: test each step before applying any gain.

E7. An AI assistant raised support agents' resolutions per hour by 14%, and by 34% for novices. Experienced agents gained little.
[Quarterly Journal of Economics, Generative AI at Work, 2025, study, https://www.nber.org/papers/w31161]
Use it to: anchor service gains near 14%.

E8. ChatGPT cut time on professional writing tasks by 40% and raised quality by 18%.
[Science, Experimental evidence on the productivity effects of generative artificial intelligence, 2023, study, https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/995024]
Use it to: cap gains on drafting steps.

E9. Back-office work gave the clearest returns, saving $2m to $10m a year in outsourced service and document work.
[MIT NANDA, The GenAI Divide, 2025, survey (directional), https://webberwentzel.com/News/Documents/2025/MIT1757411281972.pdf]
Use it to: point first workflows at back-office volume.

E10. Executives see about 70% of AI's value potential in core functions such as sales and manufacturing.
[BCG, The Widening AI Value Gap, 2025, survey, https://media-publications.bcg.com/The-Widening-AI-Value-Gap-October-2025.pdf]
Use it to: keep front-office ideas open as potential.

## 4. How big savings really are

E11. Across 18 transformation programs, early value estimates shrank about 70% before reaching cash flow. Not specific to AI.
[McKinsey, Keeping transformations on target, 2017, study, https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/rts/our-insights/keeping-transformations-on-target]
Use it to: plan a pipeline three times the target.

E12. Danish chatbot users saved about 3% of work hours, with no measurable effect on earnings or hours.
[University of Chicago, Large Language Models, Small Labor Market Effects, 2025, study, https://bfi.uchicago.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/BFI_WP_2025-56-3.pdf]
Use it to: show that a chatbot alone saves little time.

E13. Experienced developers took 19% longer with early-2025 AI tools, yet believed they were 20% faster. METR now calls it dated.
[METR, Measuring the Impact of Early-2025 AI on Experienced Open-Source Developer Productivity, 2025, study, https://metr.org/blog/2025-07-10-early-2025-ai-experienced-os-dev-study/]
Use it to: replace self-reported savings with timed cases.

## 5. Benchmarks by workflow

### Quoting

E14. Automotive suppliers won one bid in four and spent 134 labor hours per quote request.
[Center for Automotive Research, Automotive suppliers win only 25% of new business bids, 2002, survey, https://www.just-auto.com/news/usa-automotive-suppliers-win-only-25-of-new-business-bids-study]
Use it to: frame hours lost on failed bids.

E15. At Teknorot, an automotive supplier, machine learning estimated part costs from drawings with about 10% average error.
[Boğaziçi University, Machine Learning-Based Manufacturing Cost Prediction from 2D Engineering Drawings via Geometric Features, 2025, study, https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.12440v1]
Use it to: back AI draft estimates that estimators approve.

### Order entry

E16. Digital orders cost $2 to $6 to process, against $7 to $21 for paper. Automating entry cut cycle time 46%.
[APQC, Automating the Sales Order Entry Process, 2017, benchmark (vendor-sponsored), https://cloud.esker.com/fm/others/APQC-Survey-Automating-the-Sales-Order-Entry-Process-2017.pdf]
Use it to: size savings per order.

E17. Given a few examples, general AI models scored about 98% on invoice fields, against 78% with none.
[University of Las Palmas de Gran Canaria, Information Extraction from Electricity Invoices with General-Purpose Large Language Models, 2026, study, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.25927]
Use it to: allow touchless handling only after validation.

### Customer service

E18. Klarna's AI handled two-thirds of inquiries and cut cost per transaction 40%. In 2025 it began hiring agents again so customers can always reach a person.
[CX Dive, Klarna credits AI for slashing customer service costs, 2025, case, https://www.customerexperiencedive.com/news/klarna-credits-ai-for-slashing-customer-service-costs/748647/]
Use it to: pair cost per contact with quality measures.

E19. Gartner predicts half of firms planning big cuts to service staff will drop those plans by 2027.
[Gartner, Gartner Predicts 50 Percent of Organizations Will Abandon Plans to Reduce Customer Service Workforce Due to AI, 2025, prediction, https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-06-10-gartner-predicts-50-percent-of-organizations-will-abandon-plans-to-reduce-customer-service-workforce-due-to-ai]
Use it to: size value in contacts and handling time.

Also see E7.

### Accounts payable

E20. Best-in-class AP teams spend about $2.65 per invoice, against a $12.42 average.
[Ardent Partners, The State of ePayables 2025, 2025, benchmark (vendor-sponsored), https://tradeshift.com/state-of-epayables-2025-report/]
Use it to: size invoice savings without user cost data.

E21. Best-in-class AP teams flag about 9% of invoices as exceptions, 59% fewer than peers.
[Ardent Partners, AP Performance Advantages of Best-in-Class, 2024, benchmark (vendor-sponsored), https://payablesplace.ardentpartners.com/2024/09/ap-performance-advantages-of-best-in-class/]
Use it to: design around exceptions, aiming below 10%.

### Supplier onboarding and vendor fraud

E22. Removed. The APQC supplier setup time is undated, and its sample and start point are unknown. Do not cite it.

E23. Attempted or actual payment fraud hit 79% of US firms in 2024. Vendor impersonation rose 11 points, to 45%.
[Association for Financial Professionals, 2025 AFP Payments Fraud and Control Survey, 2025, survey, https://www.financialprofessionals.org/about/learn-more/press-releases/Details/survey-79-percent-of-organizations-were-victims-of-attempted-or-actual-payments-fraud-activity-in-2024]
Use it to: make bank-detail checks a fixed control.

### Compliance documentation

E24. At a 150-person machinery maker, red tape cost 6.3% of revenue. Larger firms carried 1.3% to 1.6%.
[IfM Bonn, Bürokratiekosten von Unternehmen aus dem Maschinen- und Anlagenbau, 2025, study, https://idw-online.de/en/news849686]
Use it to: size compliance cost as a revenue share.

E25. An AI platform cut first-draft time for regulatory summaries by about 97%. Expert writers were still needed.
[Weave and Takeda, Human-AI Collaboration Increases Efficiency in Regulatory Writing, 2025, vendor claim, https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.09738]
Use it to: back AI drafting with expert sign-off.

### Hiring

E26. In a trial with 67,000 applicants, AI voice interviews raised offers 12%, starts 18% and one-month retention 17%. People made every hiring decision.
[University of Chicago and Erasmus University Rotterdam, Voice AI in Firms, 2025, study, https://ideas.repec.org/p/arx/papers/2607.28222.html]
Use it to: back AI screening for high-volume, entry-level roles.

### Finance close

E27. Accountants using AI supported 55% more clients a week and closed monthly books 7.5 days faster.
[Stanford GSB and MIT Sloan, Human + AI in Accounting, 2025, study, https://gsb.stanford.edu/insights/ai-reshaping-accounting-jobs-doing-boring-stuff]
Use it to: size close redesign in days saved.

## 6. Firms like yours

E28. In 2025, 17% of small, 30% of medium and 55% of large EU firms used AI.
[Eurostat, 20% of EU enterprises use AI technologies, 2025, survey, https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/products-eurostat-news/w/ddn-20251211-2]
Use it to: show small-firm peers are still early.

E29. Three in four SMEs using AI are novices applying off-the-shelf tools to isolated tasks. In Japan, 23% of firm-wide users saw transformational impact, against 2% of isolated-task users.
[OECD, Empowering SMEs in the age of AI, 2026, survey, https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/empowering-smes-in-the-age-of-ai_bf5a9816-en.html]
Use it to: push owners toward one integrated workflow.

E30. Nolinor Aviation cut compliance investigation time from 40 hours to 5 with an AI assistant.
[Future Skills Centre, AI in action: SMEs, 2025, case, https://fsc-ccf.ca/research/ai-in-action-smes/]
Use it to: show a small-firm compliance case.

E31. German manufacturer EMES uses AI to turn email and PDF orders into ERP orders, with traffic-light routing. No measured gains are published.
[Mittelstand-Digital Zentrum Chemnitz, KI-gestützte Auftragserfassung, undated, case, https://digitalzentrum-chemnitz.de/downloads/praxisbeispiel_ki-gestuetzte-auftragserfassung/]
Use it to: borrow its pattern for order entry.

## 7. Testing common pet ideas

- Social media posts. Use E8 and E9, plus the user's own numbers. More leads help only when the firm can handle the requests it already gets.
- Website chatbots. Use E18 and E7. Worthwhile when many customers ask the same simple questions and a person stays reachable.
- Voice bots. Use E18, E7 and E19. Say that E18 comes from a consumer business. Worthwhile when calls are many, follow a script, and a person stays reachable.
- Supplier or employee chatbots. Use E2 and E12. Worthwhile when the bot answers from live system data and removes a counted volume of emails.
