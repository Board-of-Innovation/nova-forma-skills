# Scoring anchors for the six criteria

Score every criterion against these anchors. A total runs from 6 to 18, with Low or unknown counted as 1. When the evidence sits between two levels, take the lower one and note why.

| Criterion | Low (1) | Medium (2) | High (3) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Losing value | No visible loss today | Some loss that is hard to measure | Money you can point to is lost every month: declined or late requests, margin leakage, penalties, churn |
| Repetitive | Every case is unique | A recurring core with many variants | Most cases follow the same pattern |
| Expensive | Less than one FTE a year | One to three FTE a year | More than three FTE a year, or a large share of a scarce expert's time |
| Slow | The receiver waits minutes | The receiver waits hours | The receiver waits days, or the next team cannot start |
| Data access | Inputs live on paper, in people's heads, or out of reach | Inputs are digital but scattered, or only reachable through manual exports | Inputs and outputs are digital, in systems you can get access to |
| Sponsor | Nobody owns the result | Someone is interested but not accountable | A named person answers for the result's KPI and can decide |

## Notes on scoring

- Convert hours to FTE with about 1,700 working hours a year, unless the user gives a figure.
- For a small team, judge "expensive" against the team's size: two FTE in a team of five is High.
- Sponsor and data access are gates. A Low on either, scored on evidence, moves the candidate to "not yet", with what would lift it, such as "name an owner" or "get read access to the ERP".
- An unknown gate is not a Low. Show it as "? to confirm" and count it as 1. Keep the candidate eligible, and ask the user to confirm the gate under Where I need your input. A candidate from the standard map starts with both gates unknown.
- Score the workflow as it runs today. A planned system or reorganization does not count until it exists.

## Evidence that settles a score

| Criterion | Good evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Losing value | Declined or lost requests, discounts given away, penalties, complaint logs |
| Repetitive | A sample of 20 recent cases and how many follow the main pattern |
| Expensive | Case volume times time per case, or time records |
| Slow | Timestamps from the system of record, from trigger to result |
| Data access | Where the inputs live, and whether an agent could read them |
| Sponsor | A named person who confirms they own the KPI |
